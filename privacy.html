2. The short version

  • No cookies, no tracking. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising, or social-media tracking services.
  • Self-hosted assets. All fonts, stylesheets, scripts, and images are served from this domain. Simply visiting the site sends no requests to third parties.
  • Third parties only on your action. Data is sent to a third party only when you actively click something: playing an embedded video, opening the external sign-up page, or joining a WhatsApp group.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Example Hosting Ltd., 1 Sample Street, 10000 Sampletown, Country (“the hosting provider”). When you access the site, the hosting provider automatically processes the data that your browser transmits, and may record it in server log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, referrer URL, browser type and version, and operating system.

This data is technically necessary to deliver the website to you and to ensure the stability and security of the service (e.g., defending against abuse and attacks). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of this website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Log data is not merged with other data sources and is deleted after 14 days — confirm the hosting provider's actual log retention period. We have concluded a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) with the hosting provider.

4. Embedded videos (YouTube and Google Drive) — two-click protection

Videos on this site are not loaded until you click them. Until then, you only see a preview image served from this domain, and no data is sent to any third party.

When you click a video, your browser connects to YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) or, for the Executive Intelligence Review short film, to Google Drive. Both services are provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Google then processes your IP address and device information, and may set cookies or similar identifiers, under its own privacy policy. This may involve transfers of your data to Google LLC in the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognized as providing an adequate level of protection.

The legal basis for loading these videos is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you give by clicking the video after reading the notice shown on it. Your choice is remembered per provider in your browser's local storage (entries yt-embed-consent and drive-embed-consent), so that further videos from the same provider play without repeating the notice. These entries are not cookies, contain no identifiers, and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by deleting this site's data in your browser settings — videos will then show the consent notice again.

5. Signing up to the Youth Movement

This website itself contains no sign-up form. The “sign up” button takes you to a separate sign-up page at schillerinstitute.nationbuilder.com, which is operated on the NationBuilder platform (3DNA Corp., Los Angeles, USA). Any data you enter there (such as your name, email address, and phone number) is processed on that platform under its own privacy policy, and by us as controller to contact you about the Youth Movement and its activities.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time, for example by emailing datenschutz@schillerinstitute.com; the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal remains unaffected. We store your sign-up data until you withdraw your consent or object, at the latest 24 months after the last contact — confirm the actual retention rule.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the data you provide (your contact details and the content of your message) in order to handle your inquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your inquiry (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) or, where your inquiry is aimed at joining or a similar arrangement, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. We delete this correspondence once your inquiry is fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations require longer storage.

7. WhatsApp groups

Joining one of our WhatsApp groups is voluntary and happens on your initiative by scanning a QR code or following an invitation link. WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms) will then process your phone number and profile information under its own privacy policy, and other group members will be able to see your phone number, profile name, and messages. If you prefer not to share this data with WhatsApp, please use email or phone to contact us instead.

8. External links

This site links to external websites (for example the Executive Intelligence Review subscription page and coordinators' LinkedIn profiles). Clicking such a link takes you to a service outside our control; the respective operator's privacy policy applies there. No data is transferred to these services merely by visiting our site.

9. Your rights

With regard to your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data erased (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR), and
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at datenschutz@schillerinstitute.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement. The authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HBDI), Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany — correct if the controller is not the Wiesbaden e.V..

10. No automated decision-making

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling. You are neither legally nor contractually obliged to provide personal data to use this website; without it, you simply cannot be contacted or signed up.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when the website or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
